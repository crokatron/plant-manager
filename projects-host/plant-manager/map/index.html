Move around your home

Mouse / trackpad: scroll over the map to zoom toward your pointer. Drag empty space to pan. Space + drag or the middle mouse button pans even over a plant.

Touch: drag empty space to pan; use two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, or twist to rotate. A second finger cancels any tentative plant move.

Plant details: click, tap or hold a plant photo. Keyboard: focus a plant and press Enter.

Edit placements: drag a photo with one finger or the left mouse button, then release inside a room to save. Escape cancels; Ctrl / ⌘ + Z undoes the last move.

View: choose a room to focus, Fit home to see everything, and Drawing up to reset rotation. Photos grow gently as you zoom; labels stay upright and readable.
